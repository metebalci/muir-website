01 / WHAT IT IS

What it is

THE SYSTEMFROM MIT’S SOURCES

The Lisp Machine system

For QUUX, continuing where MIT’s released system left off: MIT’s own sources, recovered from its backup tapes and brought up by LM-3.

THE MACHINEQUUX

QUUX is the CADR evolved

In muir-sim and muir-fpga: better in performance and capacity, and in modern computing, such as the display’s resolution, block storage and the network. It says which improvements it has, and the system reads them at boot.

THE AIMSTEP BY STEP

It evolves the system

Step by step, rather than changing it radically. Backward compatibility is not an aim.

THE RELEASESFOR THE CADR

The releases for the CADR are out

Each a disk pack ready to boot, and the sources. The release notes say what each changed; get them on GitHub. The CADR’s releases continue on their own line; the updated system, for QUUX, is in progress.

IT RUNS ONSIMULATOR · FPGA

muir-sim and muir-fpga

muir-sim, the simulator, and muir-fpga, on an FPGA, with ozd as its Chaosnet services daemon for the CADR’s releases.
